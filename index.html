What is this?

This is an infinite, chunk-based desert environment generated with Perlin noise. You can explore it on foot or ride the rubber tire. It works especially well on mobile, where you can look around by moving your device. Built with Three.js as a learning project, without using a game engine or physics engine. September 2025.

Controls

Desktop:
- WASD → Move
- Mouse / Arrow Keys → Look around
- Space → Mount / Dismount the tire

Mobile:
- Single tap → Move forward
- Single tap near bottom → Move backward
- Swipe to edges → Turn around
- Gyroscope → Look around
- Double tap → Mount / Dismount the tire
- Tilt phone → Control tire orientation
