# ThreeJS Physics Simulation

A ThreeJS physics simulation where you can explore infinite procedural environments by walking, rolling, sliding, or flying.  
Control your view and your vehicle by moving and tilting your phone on mobile.

Try here: [acronert.github.io/threeJS_game](https://acronert.github.io/threeJS_game)
(Best experienced on mobile)

---

## Features

### Procedural Environments
- Generated with Perlin noise, rendered in chunks  
- Level of Detail (LOD) according to distance to preserve performance  
- Chunk size adapts with altitude to extend the horizon  
- Three environments:
  - Desert dunes  
  - Islands  
  - Snow slope (WIP)

### Vehicles
- Walking (with footsteps)  
- Rubber tire  
- Beach Ball  
- Snowboard  
- Deltaplane  
- Jet plane

### Physics
- Custom physics engine supporting sliding, bouncing, and flying
